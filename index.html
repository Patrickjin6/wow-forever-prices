How the picks work
  • Every hour a GitHub job saves Blizzard's auction house data for your Horde house and Blackwater. That history sets each item's sell price, buy limit and sales per day. Blizzard's data is about an hour behind real time.
  • Your Auctionator scan, if you drop it in and it's under 2 hours old, supplies the price right now. Otherwise Blizzard's price is used.
  • Sell price: the lower of the 24-hour and 72-hour median cheapest price, and under whatever would still be listed ahead of you.
  • Buy limit: sell price × 0.95 ÷ 1.10, so every buy clears about 10% profit after the cut.
  • History rule: a buy needs at least two dips to the buy limit in the last week, at least 2 out of 3 bouncing back to the sell price within 24 hours, a 7-day trend not down more than 10%, and a current dip no longer than the slowest past recovery.
  • Quantity: at most a quarter of a day's sales and 40% of your gold per item (Blackwater: at most a tenth of a day's Blackwater sales).
  • Sales per day are estimated from listings that disappear between hourly snapshots, so cancelled listings count too.
  • Holdings and gold come from holdings.json in the repo, which Claude updates when you report buys and sales.

Data: Blizzard API, collected hourly in github.com/Patrickjin6/wow-forever-prices. Reload the page for the latest.
