How the picks work
  • Blizzard's auction house data for your Horde house is saved every hour on GitHub. That history sets each item's sell price, buy limit and sales per day. It's about an hour behind real time.
  • Your Auctionator scan supplies the price right now. If the scan is more than 2 hours old, the page uses Blizzard's price instead.
  • Sell price: the lower of the 24-hour and 72-hour median cheapest price, and under whatever would still be listed ahead of you.
  • Buy limit: sell price × 0.95 ÷ 1.10, so every buy clears about 10% profit after the cut.
  • History rule: a buy needs at least two dips to the buy limit in the last week, at least 2 out of 3 bouncing back to the sell price within 24 hours, a 7-day trend not down more than 10%, and a current dip no longer than the slowest past recovery.
  • Quantity: at most a quarter of a day's sales and 40% of your gold per item.
  • Sales per day are estimated from listings that disappear between hourly snapshots, so cancelled listings count too.
